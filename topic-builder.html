Topic Editor

Open the folder resources/topics/ from your project to begin.

Welcome

Click Open folder and select your project's resources/topics/ directory. The editor will parse every topic folder it finds and let you browse, edit, add, and remove sections, illustrations, and quiz data.

Requires a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera).
Files are written directly to disk on save. Make sure your work is committed or backed up.
